Let an assistant do it for you

Unpaid invoices piling up, forgotten reminders, cash flow getting squeezed. We set up automatic, polite and regular reminders by WhatsApp, SMS or email, based on your list of invoices.

What it covers

Every unpaid invoice is chased at the right time

You set the rhythm; the assistant takes care of it.

A reminder schedule

A reminder before the due date, one after it is late, then a firmer one: the rhythm is yours.

Polite, clear messages

Messages adapted to your customers, in French or English, with the amount, the invoice and how to pay.

You stay in control

As soon as a customer replies or promises to pay, the assistant stops and notifies you.

Simple tracking

A table of unpaid invoices kept up to date, showing what was chased and what is still owed.

Who it is for
