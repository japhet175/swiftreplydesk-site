What it covers

Your WhatsApp number answers, even when you're busy

An assistant connected to your WhatsApp Business number that answers common questions, takes simple orders, or hands off cleanly to a human when it matters.

Order taking

For a restaurant or shop: the customer sees the menu, orders and gets price confirmation, with no human in the loop.

Automatic FAQ

For a school: hours, fees and admissions answered instantly, around the clock.

Triage before handoff

Requests are qualified and sorted before reaching a human agent, who saves time as a result.

Who it's for

Your customers are already on WhatsApp

Shops, restaurants, schools and small e-commerce stores: if your customers already message you on WhatsApp, an assistant saves you answering the same questions ten times a day. In East Africa, WhatsApp is already the main sales channel — make it work for you.

Shops & restaurants

Tools & method

A solid API, an assistant that's easy to maintain

We use the official WhatsApp Business API (Twilio, 360dialog or Meta) and a simple, reliable backend that's easy to extend. For natural-language replies, we can add a lightweight AI model — only if your use case actually calls for it.
